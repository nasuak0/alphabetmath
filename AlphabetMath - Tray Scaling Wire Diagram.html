Tray scaling — how Home, Topics & Lab share one architecture

Every screen is a fixed 980×612 logical canvas (.scr) scaled to the tray by a single transform. Tiles and cartridges are children of .scr at fixed logical sizes — they scale with the tray as one unit, never layered on top of it. This is the permanent tray-containment rule.
